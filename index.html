Better for the islands, easier for you

It tastes like bottled water

Reverse osmosis takes out the salt and the chlorine, then a mineral stage adds calcium and magnesium back. Most local bottled water is made in exactly the same way.

Nothing to carry home

No more packs from the supermarket, barrel deliveries or crates in the hallway. There is always enough for drinking, cooking and coffee.

The money stays sensible

In Malta, a family typically spends about as much on bottled water as our plan costs, and the plan has no hidden extras.
